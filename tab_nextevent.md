#### Thursday, 4 June 2026 10:00am (in-person)

OWASP AI Security Summit is taking place at Infosecurity Europe 2026 on Thursday, 4 June 2026 at ExCeL London at 10am
Location inside Excel: South Gallery Rooms 18 & 19

The OWASP GenAI Security Project is partnering with Infosecurity Europe to bring together global project leaders, industry practitioners, researchers, and experts focused on securing GenAI and agentic systems at scale.

This is a unique opportunity to learn from the people shaping the future of GenAI and agentic AI security, and to connect with the OWASP community, practitioners, researchers, and industry leaders working on these challenges every day.

OWASP GenAI Security Summit:
Thursday, 4 June, 10:00 - 15:00

Register to visit Infosecurity Europe to attend the summit.
